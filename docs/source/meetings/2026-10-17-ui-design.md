## What Was Discussed

With step 1 merged, the owner chose to design what Rapidkoi draws itself (the frame, home, tabs) before links. They sketched it by hand. We worked through the new tab, how people find apps again with no search engine, pictures, and the frame with an app open.

## Decisions Made

**The frame** (sketch 2):
- The system title bar keeps the window controls.
- Rapidkoi's bar has ← ⌂ → (back, home, forward), the address/search box, then ⓘ (app info) and ⤴ (share).
- The **tab rail is on the right**: open apps as circles, the current one marked, **+** for a new tab, a divider, then settings and more at the bottom.
- Apps get only the area between the bar and the rail. They can't draw over or imitate Rapidkoi's parts (contract v1 gives them semantic components only).

**The address box:**
- On home it is a search box.
- With an app open, it shows the verified address and route, in Rapidkoi's style. A local file shows `📄 name · local file`, with no ✓.
- Clicking it turns it back into search.

**Home** (a new tab):
- The Rapidkoi logo, a **Where you left off** banner with Continue, and a **Pinned** grid of big tiles.
- Search narrows the same grid. Results look like what people remember.
- Visual, not lists: large icons, banners, grids.

**Finding things again:**
- With no search engine, Rapidkoi's memory is the search. Everything opened is remembered automatically, **on the device only**.
- Search is instant, offline and loose (`brd spr` → "Board · Sprint 3"). It remembers places (app · screen title) and where a link came from. Ctrl+K opens it from anywhere.
- There are forget and clear options.

**Pictures:**
- **No screenshots**: what someone saw is too sensitive.
- The fallback chain is: **page banner → the app's main banner → generated art** (a stable pattern and initial from the address). Its colours come from an art palette in `docs/DESIGN.md`.
- Pages can be marked `private`: they show only the app's name and main banner. "Where you left off" can be switched off.

**App info:** a **panel over the app** (the owner's choice), with name, art, address and verification, version, permissions, and Restart, Pin, Close tab. Right-clicking a tab gives the same actions.

**A crashed app** shows "stopped working" inside its tab; the other tabs carry on.

**Contract additions** (optional props on the root node, so no version bump): `title`, `banner` (an image named inside the bundle, not bytes per screen), `private`.

**DECISION-016:** option A. A disabled text field is added to kivuli (the owner asked for its session prompt).

## Constraints Introduced

- Images from bundles are untrusted files: limited formats (PNG, maybe WebP), size caps, decoded only by Rapidkoi. To record when bundles are designed (DECISION-009).

## Open Questions Raised

- "Continue" could restore the app's exact state, since Rapidkoi holds it. Saving state to disk raises size and privacy questions (PRP-003).
- Discovering new apps without a search engine (links, QR codes, maybe a directory one day): later.
