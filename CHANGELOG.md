## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Right-click for apps (PRP-070): a box's `on_context` message is sent on a right-click, a long press or the Menu key, and Rapidkoi opens nothing, so the app draws what follows (kivuli 0.68's `.on_context`; a contract 11 addition, rapidkoi-rust `.on_context`). All apps opens an app's details on a right-click on its tile.
- All apps (PRP-067, DECISION-094): every app you've opened, kept until you Forget it, in a built-in app opened from the dock's last button. Pressed while All apps is in front, the button closes it and takes you back to where you were; leaving All apps for any other app closes it. ⚙ shows its permissions. Rapidkoi's own apps show a "Rapidkoi" chip in the address bar instead of the shield; a pin (`pin.svg`) replaces ☆, and All apps itself can't be pinned. A–Z or Last used, a search box (Enter opens the first match), and where an app is from when two share a name. Each app's ⋯ shows its address, publisher and when you last opened it, and opens it, pins or unpins it, or opens Rapidkoi's own panel on its permissions or Forget. Rapidkoi keeps your apps in an `apps` file, started from Recent and your pins. Contract 11 additions for built-in apps: `apps`, `open_app`, `pin_app`, `unpin_app`, `app_permissions`, `forget_app` (built-in `read_apps` and `manage_pins`).
- The bell (PRP-066): in the header before 👤 and ☰, with the accent's dot while anything is unread. Its card lists every notification, unread first, then "Earlier" and the read ones, each with its app's icon, its sentence, and its app and when; a row opens its place and marks it read, × removes it; with none, "No notifications". Opening the card marks nothing read.
- Notifications (PRP-065, DECISION-093): an app that declares `notifications` can send you one while it runs: a sentence and a place in it, with a key that replaces or withdraws it. The first time, Rapidkoi asks (Don't allow, Allow once, Always allow); Blocked in ⚙ drops them and the app runs on. Rapidkoi keeps them in a `notifications` file (20 per app, 200 in all, at most 10 a minute from one app), with each app's name and icon; Forget removes an app's. The new tab's Notifications is real: only while something is unread, its count in the accent, then the newest four, unread first; a row opens its place and marks it read, × removes it. Contract 11 additions: `notify`, `withdraw`, and the new tab's `notifications`, `open_notification` and `dismiss_notification` (built-in `read_notifications`). The counter example has "Notify me".

### Changed
- An app's start as one pipeline (PRP-069, DECISION-096): Source, Checked, Compiled, Started (`src/boot/appboot.la`), whether built in, a local file or a signed site; what it asks is answered by Settle. Every app compiles through the modules kept for the run, so a file or site opened again compiles nothing, and a changed one compiles fresh. `tabs.open_path` became `appboot.opened`.
- Rapidkoi's start as its stages (PRP-068, DECISION-096): setup, the data folder, restore (a list of parts, `data/restoring.la`), the session, the built-ins, the first window, then Settle (`src/boot/`). Settle is one set of passes after start and after every message, so start now also tells apps what changed and fetches the pictures they name, as updates do. Compiled modules are kept for the run, keyed by their bytes' SHA-256 (at most 16): the new tab, Settings and All apps compile once, however often they open.
- Every × is Rapidkoi's own `x.svg` (`Icon.Close`): a tab's, an instance's, the notice bar's, the bell's rows', and the new tab's notifications' (packed as its asset, in the faint grey); GTK's `window-close-symbolic` and the letter × are gone.
- The new tab no longer shows Pinned or See all (PRP-067): your pins are the dock, and All apps has every app; it no longer declares `read_pins`.

### Fixed
- The new tab's × on a notification did nothing: it was a text, which Rapidkoi doesn't make pressable (now a box); and an app's own change (dismissing) is told to it in the same update, not the next.
- A recent place recorded before the first tick showed "1 Jan 1970": Rapidkoi reads the clock at launch, and the new tab leaves out a time it doesn't know (0) and an empty address; Rapidkoi's own apps answer with no address (`builtin:settings` was shown).

### Added

Settings opens by its path (`builtin:settings`) from a pin, a recent card or the address box, as ☰ opens it. "Show in Recent" is its own ⚙ row (`app_settings.recent`), Settings' included.
- An app's colours (PRP-063): `[colors] primary = "#rrggbb"` in `rapidkoi.toml` (`pack --color primary=…`), kept in the manifest as `colors`; Recent's cards wash in each app's primary colour from the top-left, its icon at the top-left, the large card as the small ones; an app with none gets the dock's palette colour. The new tab and Settings name theirs. Recent places carry `colors.primary` (rapidkoi-rust `v0.5.0-pre.3`).
- Recent places replace "where you left off" (PRP-062, DECISION-044): the places you were, newest first (the newest 100, a `recent` file), each a window's address (a site's link with its fields, or a file) with its app and title. The new tab's Recent is real, each app once by its newest place: the newest large, the next four small, each with its app's icon, title, app and when, opening a fresh window there. "Show in Recent" in each app's ⚙ card; Forget removes an app's places. Continue and the left-off record are gone; a left-off file becomes a first place. Contract 11 additions: the `recent` and `open_recent` asks (rapidkoi-rust `v0.5.0-pre.2`).
- The new tab's Pinned is real (PRP-061): your pins in your order, each with its icon and name, opening its app when pressed, read again when your pins change; with no pins there's no Pinned. The address box's ☆ is filled in the brand colour while the app shown is pinned.
- Every app has an icon, and Rapidkoi's own apps show apps' icons (PRP-060, DECISION-092, contract 11): a bundle without a square PNG icon of 128 to 1,024 px, or an SVG, is refused, and `pack` and `publish` say how to add one (a bare `.wasm` still runs, as its initial). Built-in apps show any app's icon with `icon:PLACE`, drawn by Rapidkoi from what it holds, or the app's initial; no other app may. The `pins`, `left_off` and `session` answers name icons instead of carrying their bytes, and the session lists each app once with its name; the session file keeps its apps' names and icons. The new tab's Restore is real: it shows your last session's apps' icons, three then "+N", and restores. Settings and the new tab have icons. The SDK is rapidkoi-rust `v0.5.0-pre.1`.
- The new tab is a still copy of its target design (A5, `docs/designs/newtab-target.md`), filling the window's width: every name, place and notification is a placeholder, it asks Rapidkoi for nothing, and nothing on it does anything yet. What contract 9 can't draw (gradients, layers) is bundle pictures.
- Contract 10 (PRP-059, DECISION-091): apps can draw a `gradient` over a box's background (linear, or an ellipse sized and placed as fractions of the box, so a colour washes in from a corner), and lay a box's children in `layers` (each where its `place` says: a badge on a face), in a `wrap` that re-flows as the window changes (`equal`, `per_line`), or in a `fit` row that shows as many as fit. Contract 9 apps are refused with "This app needs to be rebuilt for this Rapidkoi". Built on kivuli 0.67; the SDK is rapidkoi-rust `v0.4.0-pre.1`.
- Open App… (PRP-058): ☰'s first item, and Ctrl+O, open the system's chooser ("Open an app", showing Rapidkoi apps: `.rkb` and `.wasm`); the chosen file opens as typing its path does.
- Project scaffold: a Lugha program with kivuli as its UI dependency.
- The shell: `./main app.wasm` runs a WebAssembly app under UI contract v1 (text, button, input, column, row).
- Apps that crash, loop forever, misbehave or are incompatible show "This app stopped working" with Restart, and never take Rapidkoi down.
- A Rust library for writing Rapidkoi apps, and a counter demo app.
- Rapidkoi's frame: a bar (back, home, forward, an address and search box, app info, share) and a tab rail on the right.
- Tabs: several apps at once, each in its own sandbox; a crash stays in its tab. Home isn't a tab: + and ⌂ show it, and every app stays open.
- Home: "Where you left off" (Continue returns to the app, or reopens a closed one exactly as it was) and Pinned apps, filtered as you type; Ctrl+K jumps to search.
- The app info panel: what the app is, its permissions (none), and Restart, Pin and Close tab.
- Apps can give each screen a title; Rapidkoi shows it in the tab and the window title.
- The bar lives in the title bar, one row with the window controls; Rapidkoi opens maximized, in its dark theme.
- The window is one flat frame colour, the title bar and the rail included, with no shadow; the page sits flush inside it, curving only at its top-right corner.
- Text fields an app gives no handler show greyed out instead of failing.
- Bundles: `./main pack` packs an app (its module, name, version, description, icon, banners and assets) into one `.rkb` file; type its path, or run `./main app.rkb`, to open it. The info panel shows its version and description.
- A bundle's icon shows on its tab and its home tile. Icons and banners are checked for size before anything decodes them.
- Local development: `./main serve app.rkb` serves a bundle on this machine, and `rapidkoi://localhost:8000` opens it, marked development; Reload picks up a repack.
- The info panel, redesigned: the app's icon, name, version and where it's from; Your settings (Account, Notifications, Access); On this device (Where you left off, Data); and Forget this app, each with its own page. Start over and Forget ask first.
- ⟳ Reload and ☆ Pin in the bar. Reload keeps the app's state.
- Text starts at the left, in apps and in Rapidkoi's own screens.
- Signed apps, published locally: describe an app in `rapidkoi.toml`, make a key with `./main keygen`, and `./main publish` writes a static site of signed builds and a signed index. `./main serve site/` serves it, and Rapidkoi verifies the key, the index and the build before running anything, marked ✓ verified (development). A failed check is refused with its reason and no way past it.
- A site whose key changes without its old key's approval asks first: keep using your version, or open it as a separate app. Key rotations signed by the old key are followed silently.
- `./main publish --withdraw VERSION --reason TEXT` withdraws a version; Rapidkoi refuses a withdrawn build with the publisher's reason.
- `./main pack` with no arguments packs from `rapidkoi.toml`, signed when it has a key. Signed `.rkb` files opened by path show ⚠ not confirmed until their key is trusted this session.
- `./main keygen` writes the secret key readable by you only (0600), whole or not at all.
- Rapidkoi remembers across restarts: the sites it trusts (so a swapped key still asks first), your pins, and where you left off. A signed app keeps its state by its host and key: opened again by its address, pin or Continue, it picks up where it was, and a key rotation keeps it. Unsigned apps start fresh after a restart. Each change is saved at once, in `~/.local/share/rapidkoi/`, safely: a crash leaves the old file or the new one. A file that can't be read is set aside with a notice; one from a newer Rapidkoi is left alone.
- Apps at an address load in the background: the tab opens at once on a loading page (a spinner, then the time so far), and the window stays usable meanwhile. Each request may take 30 seconds. Nothing shows ✓ until every check has passed.
- Remote apps: `rapidkoi://counter.dafifi.net` (or `https://…`) opens a signed app from a real host over HTTPS, its certificate always checked, marked ✓ verified. The first key is trusted quietly and remembered; About says since when. Remote apps must be signed; plain http, IP addresses and redirects are refused.
- The info panel opens over the app, which dims behind it; a click outside or Escape closes it. Right-click a tab for Reload, Pin, About, Close and Forget; right-click a home tile for Open, Unpin and Forget. Home's pinned apps re-flow in a grid as the window resizes, and panel rows are clickable anywhere.
- Identity: each signed app gets its own key for you, made on this computer and signed in automatically, so apps can't tell they share a person; the Account page signs out, starts over as someone new, and holds your name, which apps can show (`{FIRST_NAME}`, `{LAST_NAME}`) without ever seeing it. The app contract is now version 2: one `handle(state, event)` for everything that happens (a fresh start, every open, the app's messages, Rapidkoi's answers), so an app reopened from its saved state still asks who you are. The Rust library and the counter use it; v1 apps no longer open.
- Apps reaching the network: a signed app can make HTTPS requests to its own host and the servers it declares (`[network] servers` in `rapidkoi.toml`, `pack --server`), signed into its manifest. They're sent in the background, at most 4 at once, with no cookies kept and no redirects followed; anything else is refused before it leaves the computer, with a plain reason the app gets back. Plain HTTP reaches only this computer, and only for apps from it. The Access page lists each server with a switch, marks ones an update added as new, and an update that adds one says so once. Apps can sign in to their own server with the person's key (`docs/server-sign-in.md`; the Rust library's `SignIn`). Rapidkoi looks up every server name itself, both for an app's requests and for opening an app at an address. A name that points to this computer or the local network (private, link-local, shared ISP space, multicast and reserved ranges; IPv4 hidden in IPv6 included) is refused with a plain reason before anything is sent. Connections go only to the addresses it checked.
- Rapidkoi's new-tab app (PRP-027 Part 1): `guest/rust/newtab`, showing the wordmark, packed unsigned into `assets/apps/newtab.rkb` by `scripts/newtab.sh`. Empty tabs run it once Lugha can embed files (DECISION-038).
- The sidebar on kivuli 0.54 (PRP-028): every open tab shows its × while hovered; + sits right after the last tab until they scroll; the current tab is scrolled into view when it changes.
- Empty tabs run the new-tab app, built into `./main` with Lugha's `include_bytes` (PRP-027 Part 2).
- Rapidkoi's icons are built in from `assets/icons/` with `include_str` (PRP-029): the files are the only copy.
- Contract 3 and permissions (PRP-030): events named `on_` on the wire, `on_resume` when an app's tab comes back to the front, and a manifest's `permissions`. Built-in ones only for apps compiled into `./main`; an external app declaring one doesn't run, and one asking undeclared is stopped. The new tab reads your pins and where you left off, with Continue and a button per pin. `./main pack --permission`. Contract 2 apps still run.
- Permission settings (PRP-031): each permission an app declares is Always allowed, Needs approval or Blocked, on a Permissions page under App settings. Built-in ones start allowed. Needs approval asks in Rapidkoi's own dialog (Don't allow, Allow once, Always allow), and Blocked answers with nothing. A built-in app's manifest `controls` choose its address-box icons and card rows: the new tab shows ⚙ and share, and ⚙ opens its Permissions. `on_change` tells the app in front when what it reads changes. `./main pack --control`.
- No back, home or forward in the header (PRP-032, DECISION-041): moving around inside an app is the app's own UI.
- Named instances (PRP-033): an app can be open more than once, each instance with its own name and state (a signed app's saved under the instance). The header's strip switches between them, + opens another, and Rename… names one. The sidebar is a dock, one entry per app with a count.
- Reopening what was open (PRP-034): closing Rapidkoi keeps its open instances, their names, their order and the one in front. Starting again reopens them, signed ones with their state (now saved for every open signed instance as it changes), unsigned ones fresh. Continue to an app that's open again shows it.
- Sessions and app windows (PRP-035): launch lands on the new tab with "Restore previous session", which brings back the last session's windows as they were. Opening anything else drops it. Closing an extra window ends it; an app's data outlives its windows, and a closed app reopens where you last were. The new tab's `restore_session` permission (built-in). `window/pins.la` split from `window.la`.
- Sessions, the owner's model: "Restore previous session" stays on offer all session and stacks the old session under the current one. An unrestored one is replaced at the next launch by the session after it, unless that was empty.
- State lives with its window (session 189): every window is the same; closing one deletes its state; opening an app anew starts fresh; only Restore brings a saved window back.

- Links (PRP-036, contract 4): an app names which of its state's fields go in its window's address, and the address box shows the whole link, version included (`https://counter.dafifi.net/@0.3.0?count=3`). Opening a link opens a new window of its app at that version, with those fields set; a version no longer published opens the current one and says so. The counter's count is in its address.
- `./main publish` writes a landing page, served for every other path on the app's site: a link opened in a browser shows the app, its publisher and Open in Rapidkoi.
- The Rust library speaks contract 4: `#[derive(State)]`, and `Next` with typed link fields (a list can't go in an address: it doesn't compile).
- Contract 5 (PRP-037): apps build screens from boxes, texts and typing boxes, and style them freely: colours, fonts, sizes, borders, corners, shadows, spacing, and looks on hover, press and focus. Using Rapidkoi's colour and size names keeps them in step with Rapidkoi's theme. Any box can be pressed by click, Tab and Enter, or Space, and says what it is (button, checkbox, tab…). Typing boxes send Enter, and can hold several lines.
- The Rust library builds its widgets from those pieces (button, checkbox, tabs, spacer, text area), each restylable; a small "styled" example shows an app's own green beside Rapidkoi's accent.
- ☰ opens Settings, About Rapidkoi and Quit (PRP-038). Settings, a built-in app, chooses dark or light, more contrast, and text size from 80% to 200%, applied at once to Rapidkoi and every app that uses Rapidkoi's colours and sizes, and remembered.
- Each app's ⚙ card can switch it to your colours and your text size, for apps that ignore them.
- Apps can read your light/dark, contrast and text size choices, and are told when they change.
- Links can point inside an app's state (PRP-039): `?page=task&editor.task=42` opens with the task set, through named fields only.
- The Rust library: parts of an app keep their own typed messages (`#[derive(Message)]`, `.map`), nested state is kept as named fields, and link fields chain (`App::editor.then(Editor::task)`). A misspelt message no longer compiles. Contract 5 is complete.
- The tasks app (PRP-040), Rapidkoi's first real app: a list with All, Open and Done in its link, adding with Enter, ticking, a task's own screen with title, notes and Done, and Delete asked first. Built only on contract 5 and the Rust library, in Rapidkoi's colours.
- Storage (PRP-041, contract 6): every app gets its own SQLite database on this device. It sends SQL with parameters and gets rows back; a write is one transaction; its other windows hear which tables changed. Guarded by SQLite's own limits: 100 MB, a second a call. ⚙'s Data row shows its size and clears it; Forget removes it. The tasks app keeps its tasks there, so they outlast the window and a task's link opens it anywhere.
- Blobs (PRP-054, contract 9): an app keeps large bytes (photos, files, its own formats) beside its database, under keys it names, committed with its rows. A blob can belong to a row and goes when the row does. Apps read blobs back in checked pieces and list their keys. Up to 16 MB a blob, 1 GB and 10,000 blobs an app. ⚙'s Data row counts them; Clear and Forget remove them. An app's writes now run one at a time, and `changes` counts every statement of a batch.
- Blob pictures and uploads (PRP-055): an app shows a photo it stored with `image("blob:…")` and uploads a stored blob to its own servers with `fetch`'s `body_blob`, without the bytes passing through the app. Replacing a blob shows the new picture in every window of the app.
- Opening and saving your files (PRP-056): an app asks, and you choose a file in Rapidkoi's own dialog, titled with the app's name ("Open a file for Notes", "… to modify", "Save a file from Notes"). The app gets the file's name and contents, never where it is; it can save back only to a file you opened for it to modify, safely, and never over a change made elsewhere. Files up to 16 MB.

If it can't be loaded, every window keeps running what it ran (the reason is in the notice, which isn't drawn yet). A link that opens a new window of an app already open runs the version open.
- Two apps at one address (PRP-049, DECISION-064): an app is its host and its first trusted key wherever Rapidkoi remembers it. When a site's key changes with no signed hand-over, your version keeps working from its kept copy, with your data, and stops updating; its shield offers the new one. "Open as a separate app" asks for a name first, then opens the new publisher's app beside yours, with its own kept copy, data and tab. An address with several apps asks "Which app?"; pins, Continue and the saved session reopen the exact app, and Forget takes one app only. When the first publisher signs the site again, their app updates again.
- Several apps at one address, told apart (PRP-050): each app has a colour from its key. Where an address has more than one, a chip at the start of the address box shows the app's colour and name, and lists every app there with its key's fingerprint, Default and Rename. Typing the address opens the default, or asks which. The naming dialog shows the new app's colour, and the dock has an entry per app, marked with its colour.
- Caution is amber, apart from the brand's orange: the shield's caution look, and the apps' colours swap yellow for cyan so none looks like it. The chip, its list of apps, "Which app?" and the shield's "another key" message now follow the cards' layout: padded rows, aligned columns, one width. "Open as a separate app" opens the new app in place once it's named, with no new window; your version becomes where you left off, and Continue brings it back. The dock's colour dot sits on the app's icon.
- An app nothing trusted vouches for shows a pill in place of the shield: a crossed shield and the reason ("Not signed", "Not verified", "New publisher", "Key changed"), in the text's own colour. Development apps keep the tick.
- Notices show at last (PRP-051): a one-line bar between the header and the page says what happened in one sentence, with the full message on hover and × to close it. A notice about an app stays with its tab; one about Rapidkoi itself (its files, your identity) follows you and shows first. Before, every notice (a failed save of your pins, a failed upgrade) was set and shown nowhere.
- Popovers, notices and the dock no longer redraw the app in front: an app's screen is rebuilt and compared only when something it shows changes (its state, the theme or text size, your looks switches, your name, its pictures), using kivuli's `lazy`. With kivuli 0.65, a message no longer costs more because a big app is on screen, and the window's own popovers, menus and drop-downs no longer count as leaving the front, so opening one doesn't check the app's site (DECISION-067). Opening ⚙ or the shield on a big app should be as quick as on the new tab.
- An app's settings come down to Sign in and Forget (PRP-052): ⚙ shows Sign in as a switch (off, the app gets no key or signature, every run, and keeps its data; on, the same you) and Name for your name in it. Start over, Clear data, Start over as someone new, Sign out, Sign in and Sign in automatically are gone. Forget removes the app's windows, pin, data and settings, and reopened it sees someone new: before, forgetting an app handed it back the very first key it ever had. Which apps you forgot is kept under tags only this computer's master secret can work out.
- Times and your name, shown but never known (PRP-053, contract 8): an app writes a text in parts, and Rapidkoi fills in your name and times in your own zone, "14:32", "Thu 31 Oct" or "5 minutes ago", which moves on by itself. The app never learns your time zone or your name. Apps can ask for the time, in UTC. `{FIRST_NAME}` and `{LAST_NAME}` are gone, and contract 7 apps need rebuilding.
